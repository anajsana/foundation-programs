## Program Mission

Support academia to adopt open source AI tools and best practices by integrating PyTorch Foundation projects into educational programs and by promoting reproducible research aligned with the open source principles that drive PyTorch.

## Who is this program for?

- Instructors, researchers, or educators using PyTorch Foundation projects in academia
- Academic OSPO professionals supporting open source programs in universities or research centers
- Subject-matter experts in open source AI practices from academia

## Eligibility and Requirements

You should meet all of the following:

- **Academic affiliation:** university, academic lab, research institute, or an academic OSPO
- **Relevance to Open Source AI and/or the PyTorch Ecosystem scope:** content must relate to PyTorch Foundation projects and/or their ecosystem use in research and education
- **Open sharing:** willingness to publish slides/materials under an open license 

## Program Benefits

- **PyTorch Events Academic Track:** participate in the Program Committee to propose themes, and review proposals of the new academic track at major PyTorch Events
- **Speaking/presentation opportunities**: highlight academia-led projects relevant to the PyTorch Foundation’s scope
- **Visibility and recognition:** Visibility in the PyTorch newsletter and event discounts 
- **Community access:** networking with peers across academic OSPOs and research labs, engaging with the PyTorch ecosystem
  
## Ways to Engage

### 1. Present on a Topic

Submit a session for the 2026 Webinar Series. Topics are not limited, but may include:
- Academia–Foundation showcases: Presentations on PyTorch Foundation–hosted projects that originated in academia and were later donated to the Foundation
- Academic OSPO and researcher-led sessions: Projects and studies using PyTorch libraries or PyTorch ecosystem projects
- Open Source AI best practices in academia

> Please email your proposal to 📩 `ospo-outreach@pytorch.org`

### 2. Advise on Program Design and Evolution

Join the Program Committee. We meet monthly on Wednesdays and participate in ongoing discussions in the PyTorch Slack channel #ospo-and-academic-outreach.

### 3. Connect and Share with the Academic Community

Join the program mailing list OSPO & Academic Outreach mailing list: https://lists.pytorch.org/g/ospo-outreach.
Introduce yourself to the academic community and share ideas, resources, and opportunities.


## If you are a PyTorch Ambassador

PyTorch Ambassadors who are directly engaged in academic or research and meet eligibility criteria can submit a webinar proposal using [this form](https://github.com/pytorch-fdn/ambassador-program-management/issues/new/choose).
